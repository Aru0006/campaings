01 · Engagement

Sentiment is up — but it's a tale of two workplaces

Median eNPS reached +34, the highest since 2022. But the spread is enormous: top-quartile employers scored +58, while the bottom quartile sat at −6. The average hides a widening divide.

02 · Quiet disengagement

One in three are checked out — quietly

33% of employees told us they do the minimum and no more, yet only 9% of managers could correctly identify a disengaged report. The signal is there; most teams just aren't reading it.

33%

are “quietly disengaged”

They're not leaving — they've mentally left. The strongest predictors: no growth conversation in 6+ months (2.9× more likely), and a manager they'd rate below 6/10. Regular 1:1s cut the odds nearly in half.

Methodology

How the pulse was taken

The HR Pulse Report 2026 is based on a survey of 14,000+ employees and 2,000+ HR leaders across 30 industries in India, fielded in August 2026, alongside anonymised, aggregated signals from the Zimyo platform. Figures are medians unless noted; sector and quartile cuts require a minimum sample of 30 companies. No individual person or company is identifiable in this report.
