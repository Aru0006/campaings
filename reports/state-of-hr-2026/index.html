03 · AI at work

AI became an HR habit, not an experiment

Two-thirds of HR teams now use AI in at least one workflow — most often for screening, drafting, and answering employee queries. Adoption is highest in companies over 1,000 employees, but the fastest growth is in the mid-market.

67%

of HR teams now use AI weekly

Up from 29% in 2025. The most common use cases: resume screening (48%), policy & email drafting (44%), and an employee-facing HR help desk (37%). Only 12% say they have a formal AI policy in place — a governance gap the 2027 report will track closely.

Methodology

How we built this

The State of HR in India 2026 draws on anonymised, aggregated data from 2,500+ companies on the Zimyo platform, combined with a survey of 14,000+ employees fielded between June and August 2026. Figures are medians unless noted; sector cuts require a minimum sample of 30 companies. Nothing in this report identifies an individual company or employee.
