FAQ

Good to know

Who can take part in the referral program?

Anyone outside Zimyo can refer: customers, partners, consultants and alumni. Current Zimyo employees and their immediate family are not eligible for cash prizes, though they can still refer for recognition points.

How are points calculated?

You earn 100 points for every referred prospect our team verifies as genuine, meaning a real business email and a real phone number. Each company can be credited only once per season, and the board recalculates every night at 12:00 AM IST.

When does a referral count as closed?

A referral is closed once the company you introduced signs a contract with Zimyo. Verified referrals earn points toward your rank before that point.

How and when do I get paid?

You cash out per closed referral rather than waiting for the season to end. Payouts take 18 days on average from the contract being signed. Season prizes for the top three are paid separately after the board is finalised.
